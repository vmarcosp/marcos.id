# Writing

mode: Read

Audience: a visitor who already reads marcos.id and wants a longer note than a snippet.
Job: find a piece, read it comfortably, leave.
Action: open an article from the index.
Content: one markdown or MDX file per article in `/articles`, with title, description, and date.
Constraints: the Quiet Index stays fixed. No new hue, face, or shadow. Copy stays in Marcos's English.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Writing is a narrow column of prose inside the existing index, refusing the blog template of a hero, cards, tags, and a sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground, Soft Ink and Graphite, Recoleta for the title and the date, DM Sans for the note. Hairline rows on the index. No second hue, no card, no shadow.

STORY: The visitor sees Writing., opens one note, and reads it at a line length that fits the sentence. The date sits under the title. The way back is one link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The index matches Projects: title and lede in columns 1–3, the list in columns 4–8. An article opens with the Recoleta title, then the date and reading time, then the lede, then the first paragraph, all inside a 56ch measure (about 70 characters), left aligned with the header. Signature interaction: the same arrival fade-and-rise the other pages use, then stillness. Rows still shift 6px on hover from 768px up.

FORM: Extension of the Quiet Index, the only form the brief allows. Concept seed skipped: the design language was pinned and the request was to proceed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
